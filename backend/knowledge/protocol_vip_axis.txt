# AXI4-Stream VIP emit guide (ChipSutra)

## axi4_stream handshake
- TVALID/TREADY: master may wait on TREADY; timeout required.
- TDATA with TKEEP/TSTRB/TUSER/TID/TDEST/TLAST only if present in RTL.
- Packet: assert TLAST on last beat when the DUT uses packets.
- Reset: aresetn; idle TVALID=0 after reset.

## axis scoreboard
- Queue of beats or packet assembler; compare sink outputs / accepted beats.
- UVM: txn tdata/tlast/tkeep; monitor writes on handshake fire (VALID&&READY).
