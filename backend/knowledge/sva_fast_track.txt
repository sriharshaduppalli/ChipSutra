## Concurrent vs immediate
- Protocol/safety: concurrent `assert property (@(posedge clk) disable iff (!rst_n) ...);`
- Point-in-time checks: immediate `assert (expr) else $error("...");` inside procedural code.
- Label every concurrent directive (`a_req: assert property ...`).

## Multiclock and handshake
- One sampled clock per property unless the DUT has two named clocks.
- Req/ack: hold req until ack; bounded `##[1:N] ack` for formal.
- Do not invent a second clock domain.

## AI-assisted SVA
- Bind only to parsed DUT ports.
- Fail action `else $error("...")` on asserts; covers need no fail AB.
- Skip tautologies (`|-> 1'b1`) when the spec has a real requirement.
